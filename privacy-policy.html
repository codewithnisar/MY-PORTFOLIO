01 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to CodeWithNisar (codewithnisar.com), operated by Nisar Ali. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines transparently how we handle personal information, client project data, and website interaction details.

✦ Core Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing lists under any circumstances.

02 Information We Collect

We only collect data that is voluntarily submitted by you or automatically logged during ordinary website operation:

  • Contact Form Data: Full Name, Email Address, Selected Service Interest, Estimated Budget, and Project Message details.
  • Direct Communications: Email messages, WhatsApp inquiries, or initial project briefing documentation.
  • Technical Technical Logs: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, screen resolutions, referring URLs, and device type for performance analytics.

03 How We Use Your Information

Collected information is strictly utilized to deliver professional web development and UI/UX design services:

  • Project Inquiries: Responding to client proposals, providing estimates, and setting up discovery consultations.
  • Contract Execution: Fulfilling deliverables, invoicing, technical setup, and ongoing maintenance support.
  • Site Optimization: Analyzing aggregate website performance metrics to improve page load speed and user experience.

04 Data Security & Storage

We enforce industry-standard security protocols to protect your personal and project information:

  • Encryption: All web traffic is transmitted via Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) 256-bit encryption.
  • Access Control: Administrative access to submitted inquiries is strictly restricted to authorized personnel.
  • Data Retention: Inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary to complete client communication or maintain project records.

05 Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes trustworthy infrastructure providers for hosting and form processing:

  • Hosting Infrastructure: Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages for web hosting and SSL certificate delivery.
  • Form Handlers & APIs: Formspree / Web3Forms or direct secure mail relays.
  • External Portfolio Links: External links (GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, client live sites) operate under their respective privacy policies.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We maintain a minimal cookie policy. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies. Local Storage is used solely for essential preference persistence, such as keeping your chosen Dark Mode / Light Mode theme setting.

07 Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request full erasure of your contact submission logs from our active databases.
